Family favourite · ~10 min from Çamyuva

Phaselis

← Antalya trip

Walk until the beach wins.

An ancient Lycian and Roman port scattered through pine forest between three harbours. For a four-year-old it solves the central hiking problem: every few hundred metres there is another reason to keep going.

Make your own 2–5 km wander through the aqueduct, main avenue, baths and theatre, then stop at whichever harbour looks best for swimming. There is no route that needs to be completed.

Best for

Whole-family first day

Walking

≈2–5 km · easy · highly adjustable

Plan

Morning ruins → picnic/lunch → swim
