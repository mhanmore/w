Termessos

← Antalya trip

A museum visit with contour lines.

Termessos sits high in Güllük Mountain National Park. Reaching and exploring the theatre, walls, tombs and other ruins involves genuinely rough, steep walking rather than a paved archaeological circuit.

It is the ambitious option with a four-year-old and an excellent adult outing even if the total distance is modest. Carry contingency matters more than kilometre count.

Walking

Variable · steep · rough underfoot

From Çamyuva

Roughly 75–90 min

Verdict

Choose over Antalya city when everyone wants adventure rather than recovery.
