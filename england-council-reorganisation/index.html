Important limitation

The new timetable

For every area under review, elections go ahead on existing councils in May 2027, shadow elections follow in May 2028, and vesting day is now 1 April 2029. The government says every area will have an outcome by early 2027.

184 of England's 296 current districts and unitary councils sit inside one of the 21 areas. The remaining 112 — London, the metropolitan boroughs and existing unitaries elsewhere — are outside the programme.

Method and limits

Built from district building blocks

Each proposed council is assembled from the current district and unitary boundaries published on planning.data.gov.uk. Several proposals move named parishes or wards between councils; those transfers are recorded in the table but not drawn, because doing so needs parish-level schedules that were sent to councils rather than published as data.

Now reflects the government's 7 September withdrawal of the March decisions, and its 29 October announcement: minded-to decisions for five areas, withdrawal of the other nine July decisions, and a 1 April 2029 vesting date. As announced shows the 25 March and 16 July decisions with their April 2028 vesting date, and Surrey's October 2025 decision.

Council names are the working names used in announcements, not legal names. A minded-to decision is not final; any new councils also need parliamentary approval.
