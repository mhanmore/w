Method and limits

A documentary test, not a legal opinion

This is an AI-only audit of material published on local authority websites. No authority was contacted and no finding has been independently verified. An authority is marked adopted where a council decision, minutes, current constitutional material or comparably strong operational evidence supports adoption. An agenda or recommendation alone is not treated as a completed adoption.

Authorities are being researched from south to north. Each record carries a confidence rating and links back to the underlying evidence. “No published evidence found” means a search was made and nothing relevant was located; it is not a finding that the authority has not adopted the scheme.

The National Scheme took effect on 31 October 2026. The government’s guidance says authorities should align their constitutions and delegation arrangements with it. London’s 33 authorities are covered in more depth in the London review.
