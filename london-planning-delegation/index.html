Method and limits

A documentary test, not a legal opinion

This is an AI-only audit of material published on local authority websites. No authority was contacted and no finding has been independently verified. An authority is marked adopted where a council decision, minutes, current constitutional material or comparably strong operational evidence supports adoption. An agenda or recommendation alone is not treated as a completed adoption.

Each record carries a confidence rating and links back to the underlying evidence. “Medium” generally means that official material strongly indicates adoption but the clearest final resolution or revised constitutional text was not located in the indexed record.

The National Scheme took effect on 31 October 2026. The government’s guidance says authorities should align their constitutions and delegation arrangements with it. Records may be updated after this snapshot.
