Göynük

← Antalya trip

Two completely different days.

At the bottom, Göynük is an easy family outing into a dramatic limestone gorge. Above it, the Lycian Way becomes proper mountain terrain and connects into the Kuzdere/Gedelme uplands.

That makes it useful twice: scout the lower canyon with everyone, then return for an adult-only stage if conditions and enthusiasm line up.

Family version

Lower canyon · short and flexible

Adult version

≈12–13 km upland stages can involve substantial descent and rough terrain.
