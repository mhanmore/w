Yanartaş

← Antalya trip

A destination worth climbing to.

Natural methane flames have burned from vents in the rocky hillside for centuries. The approach is short enough to be family-sized but rough and stepped enough to feel like a real expedition.

Pair it with an easy Çıralı beach day and go up towards dusk: cooler walking, more dramatic flames, and a very obvious objective for small legs.

Best for

Family afternoon / dusk adventure

Terrain

Rocky uphill path and steps

Adult extension

Ulupınar → Yanartaş → Çıralı or the 15.3 km Komoot loop.
