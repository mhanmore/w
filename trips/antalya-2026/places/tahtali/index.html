Tahtalı

← Antalya trip

Two kilometres up before lunch.

Tahtalı rises almost directly behind the resort coast. The cable car makes the summit accessible as a family spectacle while the surrounding Lycian Way provides much more serious walking for another day.

Treat summit conditions as mountain weather regardless of what breakfast felt like at sea level. This is the day to move around for cloud and visibility rather than obeying the itinerary.
