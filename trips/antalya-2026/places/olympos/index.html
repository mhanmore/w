Headline adult hike · linear

Musa Dağı → Olympos

← Antalya trip

Mountains → ruins → Mediterranean.

Start around Adrasan, climb through the Musa Dağı section of the Lycian Way and descend towards Olympos. It is the strongest fit for the requested “big but doable” day because the finish is a destination rather than merely the car park.

The mixed-group logistics are unusually good: walkers start at Adrasan while the non-hiking party has a Çıralı/Olympos day, then everyone meets near the finish for sea, food and transport home.

Scale

≈15–16 km · ≈700–800 m ascent · 6–8 hrs

Style

Linear · mountain trail · forest · archaeological finish
