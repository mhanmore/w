Both are a good Sunday. They're just different Sundays.

Weather is a wash between the two: both forecast mostly sunny with only isolated afternoon cumulus, and the same slim ~10% rain chance. Neither is the washout risk. The real choice is what kind of day you want — and how much of it you want to spend getting there.

The two options

Rax vs. Schneeberg, side by side.

Option 1

Rax

Fast and easy. A cable car lifts you to a broad 1,546m plateau in under 8 minutes, with room to extend the day on foot.

  1. 1Train Wien Hbf / Meidling → Payerbach-Reichenau (~70 min, hourly REX/regional)
  2. 2Bus 341 or the RUFbus on-demand shuttle (08:30–18:00) to the Rax-Seilbahn valley station in Hirschwang
  3. 3Cable car: 6–8 minutes to the 1,546m plateau
Door to summit, one way≈ 1h 15m
  • Broad, walkable plateau with hut gastronomy at the Ottohaus
  • Views over the Vienna Basin, across to the Schneeberg
  • Optional extension hike to Heukuppe (2,007m) for a bigger day
  • A favourite excursion of Sigmund Freud and the Habsburgs

Option 2

Schneeberg

Higher, and the journey is the point. A cog railway climbs through forest and alpine meadow to just below Lower Austria's highest peak.

  1. 1Train Wien → Wiener Neustadt, transfer to Puchberg am Schneeberg — or a weekend direct train (~1h30–1h40m total)
  2. 2Schneebergbahn cog railway: 40 min (modern "Salamander") or 90 min nostalgic steam (seasonal), to Hochschneeberg station (~1,795–1,800m)
  3. 3Short walk/climb on to Klosterwappen (2,076m)
Door to summit station, one way≈ 2h 10–20m
  • Hochschneeberg station is the trailhead itself
  • Klosterwappen (2,076m) — the highest point in Lower Austria
  • Fischerhütte, Lower Austria's highest mountain hut, just below the summit
  • Long views reportedly reaching the Neusiedler See

The short version

Weather won't decide this. Time budget will.

Pick Rax if…

you want more time actually on the mountain, a fast and easy ascent, and the option to stretch the day with a walk to Heukuppe without losing half the day to travel.

Pick Schneeberg if…

reaching Lower Austria's highest point matters, and the cog-railway ride itself — through forest and alpine meadow — is part of the day you want, not just a means to the top.
